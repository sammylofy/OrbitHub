About CyberHub Solutions

Incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (RC 8221971)

Who We Are

CyberHub Solutions and Systems Ltd is a diversified enterprise providing end-to-end engineering, software solutions, and consultancy services in Information Technology and Telecommunications engineering across Nigeria and Africa.

Managed by a dynamic team of innovators and certified engineering experts, we deliver superior returns to our clients through cost-effective, value-oriented workforce development and infrastructure deployment.

Our Corporate Foundation

Formally registered and incorporated in January 2025 (RC: 8221971), we specialize in full automation of company processes, network service provision, fiber optics, radio frequency engineering, and capacity building.

Our unwavering commitment to our watchwords—Partnership, Relationship, and Commitment to Excellence—drives our success across every project.

Our Vision

To be a trusted resource partner and provide value-added services for the IT and Telecommunications segment in the African market, innovating to deliver value and enhance customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

We are committed to enhancing ultimate customer satisfaction by focusing on providing solutions that will move businesses in leaps and bounds through cutting-edge technology and precision execution.
